How the course fits together

Each stage uses the ideas before it. Start at the left, and click a stage to see the definitions it introduces. The arrows mean "you need this before that," not "this proves that."

The course also teaches proof methods and the field axioms, which run through every stage. This map shows what depends on what, not the order of a formal proof.

Definitions, one at a time

Each card goes in four steps: the plain meaning, the symbols broken into parts, a worked example and a non-example, and a question to check yourself.

What kind of question is this?

Answer one question at a time. Each answer narrows the question until you reach a move to try. The trail at the top shows the path you took.

When you're stuck: six steps

Work through the steps in order. Tick each one as you go. If you've been stuck for more than a few minutes, go back to step 3 and ask which condition you haven't checked.

Questions to ask yourself

  1. What exactly is given, and what do I have to show or find?
  2. Which definition applies, and what are its conditions?
  3. What does this sentence mean in ordinary words?
  4. Can I test a small case to see what is happening?
  5. Am I assuming a picture proves a general claim?
